Money Mechanics

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

Money Mechanics (“the App”) is a personal content-automation tool operated by the owner of the Money Mechanics YouTube channel. The App generates short educational finance videos and publishes them to the owner’s own YouTube channel. This policy explains what information the App accesses through Google services and how that information is handled.

Who operates the App

The App is operated by an individual creator for the sole purpose of managing their own YouTube channel. It is not a multi-user service and does not offer accounts or logins to other people.

Information the App accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the App, it requests access to their Google Account through Google’s OAuth system, limited to these YouTube Data API permissions:

  • Upload videos to the owner’s YouTube channel.
  • View and manage the owner’s YouTube account and videos (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and privacy status).

The App only accesses the YouTube account of the person who authorizes it. It does not access that person’s Gmail, their personal Google Drive files, their contacts, or their location, and it does not access data belonging to any other person.

Separately, the App uses a Google service account to read and write a single Google Sheet, owned by the channel owner, that the App uses as its internal job queue. This is the owner’s own Sheet and holds only the App’s production records; no other person’s data is involved.

How the App uses this access

The access is used solely to upload the owner’s own generated videos to the owner’s own channel and to set each video’s title, description, tags, thumbnail, and privacy status. The App does not use Google user data for advertising, does not build user profiles, and does not use the data for any purpose other than publishing the owner’s content.

Video scripts are drafted by an AI language model through the Anthropic API. No Google user data is sent to that model.

Data storage and security

  • The App runs locally on the owner’s own computer.
  • The Google authorization token is stored locally on that computer and is used only to connect to the YouTube API. It is not transmitted to any third party.
  • The App does not operate a public server that stores Google user data.

Data sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. The only recipient of uploaded content is YouTube itself, at the direction of the channel owner.

Google API Services Limited Use

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and revoking access

The locally stored authorization token can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access granted to the App can also be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children’s privacy

The App is an internal content-publishing tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.
